## Adjudication

Both arms have 68 exact passes, one independently certified bounded numerical relation, and 30 Uncovered results. Across arms all 99 native schemas agree; all 98 non-numerical-exception queries have identical raw values, multiplicities and row sequences. The remaining floating exception has separate complete-relation certificates for both arms. This is evidence of no new observed native result difference, not proof that the cross-engine comparison contract is complete.

Primary uncovered categories: exact_type_domain_mismatch: 12, order_comparator_uncovered_or_raw_numeric_difference: 7, uncovered_output_identity: 11.

- Exact type-domain mismatches include NUMERIC versus HUGEINT, and BIGINT versus HUGEINT. Equal values do not silently make their declared types interchangeable.
- Uncovered output identities include star/subquery expansion, qualified display labels and identifier case. No implicit ordinal mapping or blanket case/name erasure is used.
- ORDER BY gaps include aggregate expressions with quoted aliases, CASE/grouping keys and arithmetic keys. Raw numeric key digests can also differ across declared type domains; no output tolerance is used to excuse them.
- The numerical exception remains a raw exact failure; acceptance is solely under its preregistered independent arithmetic and relational contract. General floating WHERE/HAVING, ORDER BY/LIMIT and outside-domain inputs remain uncertified.
